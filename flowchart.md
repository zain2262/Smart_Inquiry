
# SmartInquiry — Day 1 Workflow

## Project Goal
SmartInquiry classifies customer inquiries and prepares editable reply drafts for human review. It does not automatically send replies.

## Workflow
1. Customer enters an inquiry.
2. Validate the input.
3. Classify the inquiry as Pricing, Order, Support, or Other/Uncertain.
4. Select a suitable reply template.
5. Prepare an editable reply draft.
6. Send the draft for human review.
7. A human reviews and edits the draft.
8. Mark the draft as approved or request clarification.

## Safety Rule
No customer reply is sent automatically.
